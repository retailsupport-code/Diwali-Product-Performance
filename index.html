How the rating works
  1. Too early – not inwarded, no production, or less than 7 days since Inward Date.
  2. Three measures are compared with the median of the same category (Product column):
    • Sell-through pace – Sale ÷ Production, per 30 days since inward (40%)
    • Velocity – units sold per day since inward (35%)
    • Volume – total units sold (25%)
    Above the category median overall → Fast; around it → Steady; well below it → Slow.
  3. Small batches (fewer than 20 units, or under 30% of the category's median volume) can't be Fast – they're held at Steady.
  4. Categories with fewer than 5 rated colours use the all-category median.
  5. A style with several colours gets the production-weighted average of its colour scores.
